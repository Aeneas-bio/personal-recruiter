# Default job boards

Use this list as a starting proposal during onboarding, not a fixed answer. Always ask two follow-up questions before finalizing: "Any boards you already use or trust that aren't here?" and "Is there an industry-specific board for your field?" (there almost always is one — it's frequently where the best senior-level, salary-transparent postings live).

## Generic core (propose these to everyone)

- LinkedIn Jobs — https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/
- Indeed — https://www.indeed.com/
- Glassdoor — https://www.glassdoor.com/Job/index.htm
- ZipRecruiter — https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Search-Jobs-Near-Me
- SimplyHired — https://www.simplyhired.com/

## Industry-specific examples (ask which applies, don't assume)

These exist to jog the person's memory — most fields have at least one specialized board that outperforms the generic ones for senior roles. Examples to mention if relevant:

- Life sciences / biotech / pharma — BioSpace (https://jobs.biospace.com/)
- Tech / startups — Built In (https://builtin.com/), Wellfound (https://wellfound.com/)
- Academia / research — HigherEdJobs, specific professional-society job boards, O*NET (https://www.onetonline.org/) as a role/keyword reference rather than a listings source
- Government / public sector — USAJobs (https://www.usajobs.gov/), relevant state career portals (e.g., a state government Workday instance)
- Nonprofit — Idealist (https://www.idealist.org/)
- Finance — eFinancialCareers
- Legal — LawCrossing, legal-specific bar association boards
- Healthcare / clinical — Health eCareers

If the person names a niche board that isn't in this list at all, that's fine and expected — add it as-is. The generic boards exist as a floor, not a ceiling.

## A note on search mechanics

Some boards (BioSpace is a good example) support a clean, scannable search-results URL pattern like `https://jobs.biospace.com/jobs/?keywords=KEYWORD&sort_by=date` — worth discovering and reusing during the first scan run, since it's much faster than clicking through a search form every time. LinkedIn and most ATS-hosted postings (Greenhouse, Workday, Lever) usually require opening individual postings rather than bulk-scanning a results page — budget more time per lead found on those.


## Logins, profiles & paid/gated boards

Two things materially improve results and should be part of board selection (see the `job-scan-setup` skill's board step):

- **Log in and complete a profile on each board kept.** A current, complete profile improves targeting and, on several boards, unlocks postings that logged-out or bare accounts never see. All logins happen in the person's own browser via the Claude extension — never collect, type, or store their credentials.
- **Paid / subscription / niche boards are welcome as user additions.** If the person subscribes to an executive or industry-specific board (e.g., a paid aggregator), add it — it is read through their logged-in session only, with a "needs sign-in" fallback when no session is active. Note any per-board ToS considerations, and dedupe hard, since paid aggregators often re-surface roles also found on company sites and free boards.

Always let the person **add** boards not listed here and **remove** any they don't want before the list is locked in.
